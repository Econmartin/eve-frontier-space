Asteroid Lab

EVE Frontier composite asteroid generator, ported from the client (cycle-7). Same hash, noise, shapes, prefixes and placement rules. The server-only part is which materials a belt gets.
Body
Game defaults: dim 17, radius 6, noise 1.6, spacing 35 m. Server recipes may override any of them per asteroid type.
Materials
Layers are matched by depthLo (deepest first). In each layer the heaviest massive material is the matrix; others are placed by morphology: disseminated (hash scatter), vein (winding fbm band), nodular (fbm blobs, self_affinity 0..1), massive (smooth fbm). Weight × layer voxels = target count.
